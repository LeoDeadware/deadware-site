What we did to it

  • A rock-solid 60 fpsThe game steps in time with the display. Walls and signs don’t shake.
  • Four times sharper, 16:104x render, full screen with no black bars: menus, races, HUD and loading screens.
  • HD texturesOver 5,000: ours redrawn plus five community packs from Nexus Mods and an 8K night sky.
  • A clean pictureFXAA anti-aliasing, 16x anisotropic filtering, car reflections, glowing neon and headlights.
  • Fully in UkrainianMenus, hints and messages. Original voices.
  • A native Mac appBuilt from your disc in a few minutes. Any gamepad.

We raise them for our own people — the ones who

  • Grew up so broke they dreamed of sneakers without holes instead of a console.
  • As kids drooled over pirated game discs, but those were expensive and in Russian.
  • Grew up into stuck-up hipsters with pretty MacBooks and every console under the sun.
  • But then the Russians started bombing the power grid, and now a hot shower is a luxury. And electricity… what electricity? I never saw much of it until I was a grown-up.

Long story short, this project is for you, my young grandpas and grandmas.

Grab your fucking energy-efficient MacBook and play, no power needed, the upgraded versions of games you couldn’t afford as kids.

FAQ for nerds

Is this even legal?

We don’t hand out games. You take your own copy, the port builds a native Mac app from it. If Warner Bros lawyers do show up in trench coats and dark glasses — we’ll take it down within a day, and the pig will hang around a bit longer.

Will it run on an Intel Mac?

No. Only M1 and newer. Yes, it hurts. No, we won’t change our minds.

Can I donate?NO, I’M PROUD

No. Want to help — tell a friend or star us on GitHub. Nice for us, free for you.
