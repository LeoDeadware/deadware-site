Need for Speed Underground 2 on Mac

The same 2004 game that made half the country glue neon to the bottom of their cars. Now the Xbox version drives on your Mac: a steady 60 fps, a picture four times sharper, English and Ukrainian.

Download from GitHub

You need: a Mac with an Apple chip, macOS 26 or newer, and your Xbox disc (US edition).

What changed

  • A rock-solid 60 fpsThe game steps in time with the display: every frame on time, no stutter at speed. Walls and signs no longer shake as you fly past.
  • Four times sharperThe picture renders four times larger than on the Xbox and stays sharp even on Retina.
  • 16:10 with no black barsMenus, races, HUD and loading screens fill the whole display, including the area around the notch.
  • Over 5,000 HD texturesWe redrew 4,894 textures ourselves and took the rest from five community packs on Nexus Mods. The night sky is 8K.
  • Cars hold up at a distanceTraffic, rivals and parked cars keep full detail four times farther out than on the Xbox. No more bars of soap on the horizon.
  • A clean pictureFXAA anti-aliasing, 16x anisotropic filtering, no shimmering road, car reflections and glowing neon and headlights.
  • LauncherLanguage, 60 or 120 fps, gamma (Original, Modern, Warm), bloom, 2x draw distance, motion blur.
  • Fully in UkrainianMenus, hints, messages and the launcher. Original voices.
  • A native Mac appNo emulator: built from your disc in a few minutes. Any gamepad; hold A to skip videos.

How to install

What you’ll need

  • A Mac with an Apple chip and macOS 26 or newer
  • Xbox disc image, US edition
  • A gamepad

  1. 1Prepare a disc image.US edition for the original Xbox.

A bit of history

2004. EA puts out the sequel to “Underground”, and instead of tracks it hands you a whole night city, Bayview: drive anywhere, hunt for races, carry the money to the garage. Neon, vinyls, hydraulics, all of it to “Riders on the Storm” with Snoop Dogg.

A whole generation learned the price of nitrous by heart and still hasn’t forgiven EA for never doing it again. Meanwhile the Xbox version lay in the black box under the TV, waiting for someone to remember.

Same city, same races, same garage. We just gave it a steady 60 fps, a picture four times sharper, HD textures and a Ukrainian translation.

Also known as: Need for Speed Underground 2, NFSU2, NFS Underground 2, NFS UG2, Underground 2.

FAQ for nerds

Can I play NFS Underground 2 on a Mac?

Yes, the Xbox version. The installer turns an image of your disc into a regular Mac app.

Do I need an emulator?

No, it’s a regular Mac app.

How long does the install take?

A few minutes if Apple’s developer tools are already on your Mac. If not, the installer offers to install them, and that takes longer.

What about a keyboard?

No, gamepad only. Racing on arrow keys is like drifting a shopping trolley.

Which gamepad works?

Xbox, PlayStation or another modern Mac gamepad.

Is there Ukrainian?

Yes, with a switch in the launcher: menus, hints and messages in Ukrainian. The voices are the original ones.

Where do I turn off motion blur?

In the launcher, the Motion blur row. It’s off by default: the picture stays sharper.

Whose HD textures are these?

We redrew most of them. The rest come from Remastered Underground 2 (instanity), Redux HD and Next-Gen Textures (Awesome Mods), Project Revitalized (QTmodz), and the sky from 8K Realistic Night Skyboxes (Awesome Mods). Author against it? Write to us and we’ll remove the pack within 24 hours.

Will the European edition work?

We haven’t tested it yet. The installer will tell you if something’s off.

Will it run on an old Intel Mac?

No. It’s built for Apple chips; it won’t launch on Intel.

The Mac won’t open the game?

Right-click NFSU2 → “Open” → “Open” again. We don’t pay Apple for the right to be trusted, so it gets a little nervous.
