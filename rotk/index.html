The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King on Mac

The original Xbox game, rebuilt as a native app for Apple Silicon Macs: 60 fps, your screen's native resolution, a gamepad; in Ukrainian or English. You bring your own disc.

The installer is not public yet; it will be linked here when it is.

What you get

  • A real Mac appThe Xbox game is rebuilt into a regular app for Apple Silicon Macs. Click the icon and play.
  • 60 fps, 120 on ProMotionSteady 60 frames per second, and 120 on ProMotion displays.
  • Your Mac's native resolutionThe picture is rendered for your screen instead of being stretched.
  • Three colour modesOriginal, Modern and Warm.
  • English and UkrainianEnglish by default, Ukrainian with a switch in the launcher. The voices are the original ones.
  • Gamepad, skippable cutscenesXbox or PlayStation controllers, with matching button prompts. Hold A to skip a cutscene.

What you need

  • A Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer).
  • Your own disc of The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King for the original Xbox, US version, as a disc image. The game itself is not included. The European version has not been tested.
  • A gamepad: the game is made for one and does not support a keyboard.
  • About 6 GB of disk space during installation; the installed game takes about 3.8 GB.

How to install

  1. Make an image of your Xbox disc (US version).
  2. Open the Deadware installer. It is 8.5 MB and contains no game code or data: the game is built on your Mac.
  3. Point it to your disc image. If macOS asks for Apple's Command Line Tools, install them.
  4. Open the game from its icon. With the tools already installed, the build takes about a minute and a half.

Questions

Can I play The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King on a Mac?

Yes. Deadware rebuilds the original Xbox version as a native macOS app for Apple Silicon Macs, from your own disc.

Can I play with a keyboard?

No. It's a console game made for a gamepad, so you need one: Xbox or PlayStation.

Which disc do I need?

The US version for the original Xbox. The European version has not been tested.

Is there a Ukrainian voice-over?

No. The text is in Ukrainian or English; the voices are the original ones.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

No, only Macs with an Apple chip (M1 and newer).

Do I need an emulator like xemu?

No. It's a regular Mac app: install it and play.
