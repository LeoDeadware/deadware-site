The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King on Mac

The same Peter Jackson movie game where a shot from the film slides straight into a fight. Now the Xbox version runs on your Mac: 60 fps, your screen’s native resolution, English and Ukrainian.

Download from GitHub

You need: a Mac with an Apple chip, macOS 26 or newer, and your Xbox disc (US edition).

What changed

  • 60 frames per secondAnd the full 120 on ProMotion screens.
  • Your Mac’s native resolutionThe picture is rendered for your screen, not stretched from a 2003 TV.
  • Three colour modesOriginal, Modern and Warm. Pick the Mordor you like best.
  • Two languagesUkrainian is a switch in the launcher; the game starts in English. The voices are the original ones.

How to install

What you’ll need

  • A Mac with an Apple chip and macOS 26 or newer
  • Xbox disc image, US edition
  • About 6 GB of disk space while it installs

Step by step

  1. 1Prepare a disc image.US edition for the original Xbox.
  2. 2Download our installer.The button at the top of the page takes you to GitHub: ROTK.dmg under Releases, 123 MB. No game inside, just the recipe and our HD textures.
  3. 3Drag “The Return of the King” to Applications.The first time, right-click it → Open → Open again. The Mac will ask whether you trust us. Trust us.
  4. 4Point it to the disc image.If the Mac asks for developer tools, get them. Then about two minutes, and only once.
  5. 5Pick a language and hit Play.That ring won’t throw itself into the volcano.

A bit of history

2003. EA puts out the game of the third film, and it pulls a trick that froze whole sofas: a shot from the movie slides into the fight without a seam. Aragorn draws his sword on screen, and a second later you’re the one swinging it.

Then the consoles went up to the attic, and the Xbox version stayed in the black box under the TV, next to the cables nobody dares to throw away.

Same levels, same fights, same cutscenes. We just gave it 60 fps, your screen’s native resolution and a Ukrainian translation.

Also known as: The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King, ROTK, LOTR ROTK, LOTR Return of the King.

FAQ for nerds

Can I play Return of the King on a Mac?

Yes, the Xbox version. The installer turns an image of your disc into a regular Mac app.

How long does the install take?

About two minutes if Apple’s developer tools are already on your Mac. If not, the installer offers to install them, and that takes longer.

How much space do I need?

Installed, the game takes about 3.8 GB. While it installs it needs about 2 GB more: your Mac unpacks the disc for a while.

What about a keyboard?

No, gamepad only. The game was made for sticks, and we didn’t rebuild the controls for keys.

Which gamepad works?

Xbox or PlayStation. The button prompts on screen switch to match the one in your hands.

Can I skip the videos?

Yes, hold A. Though the cutscenes here are pieces of the film, so watching them is no sin.

Is there Ukrainian?

Yes, with a switch in the launcher. The game starts in English; both versions use the original voices.

Will the European edition work?

We haven’t tested it yet. The installer will tell you if something’s off.

Will it run on an old Intel Mac?

No. It’s built for Apple chips; it won’t launch on Intel.

The Mac won’t open the game?

Right-click “The Return of the King” → “Open” → “Open” again. We don’t pay Apple for the right to be trusted, so it gets a little nervous.
