Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets on Mac

Not the PC version you remember from the internet café, but the console one, from the Xbox: different levels, different fights, a different camera. Now on the Mac, smooth and full-screen.

Download from GitHub

You need: a Mac with an Apple chip, macOS 26 or newer, and your Xbox disc (US edition).

What changed

  • Four times sharperThe picture is four times bigger, 330 textures redrawn.
  • 60 fps and widescreenSmooth, with no black bars on the sides.
  • A modern cameraFollows the stick and gets behind Harry on its own.
  • Any gamepadXbox, PlayStation or Switch Pro. Plug it in mid-game — and it works right away, no restart.

How to install

What you’ll need

  • A Mac with an Apple chip and macOS 26 or newer
  • Xbox disc image, US edition
  • About 2.8 GB of disk space

Step by step

  1. 1Prepare a disc image.US edition for the original Xbox.
  2. 2Download our installer.The button at the top of the page downloads HP2.dmg, 127 MB. No game inside, just the recipe and our HD textures.
  3. 3Drag “Harry Potter” to Applications.The first time, right-click it → Open → Open again. The Mac will ask whether you trust us. Trust us.
  4. 4Point it to the disc image.If the Mac asks for developer tools, install them. Then the game is built on your Mac: about 20 minutes, once.
  5. 5Pick a language and hit Play.Hogwarts is waiting. So, unfortunately, is Lockhart.

A bit of history

In 2002 every platform got its own “Chamber of Secrets”, and the versions differed like brothers from different marriages. The Xbox version is a console game with its own levels and fights. It never came out on PC.

The 2003 Mac release was a port of the PC version for old Macs, and it has long been dead. So for the first time in twenty years the Xbox version lives somewhere other than the black box under the TV.

Same levels, same fights. We just gave it the widescreen, smoothness and sharpness the 2002 console didn’t have the strength for.

FAQ for nerds

The Mac won’t open the game?

Right-click “Harry Potter” → «Open» → «Open» again. We don't pay Apple for the right to be trusted, so it gets a little nervous.

How long does the install take?

About 20 minutes if Apple’s developer tools are already on your Mac. If not, the installer offers to install them, and that takes longer.

Can I play “Chamber of Secrets” on a Mac?

Yes, the Xbox version. The installer turns your disc into a regular Mac app.

What do I need to set up?

Nothing. It’s a regular Mac app: click the icon, play.

Why the Xbox version?

Because it’s a different game: its own levels, fights and camera that never existed on computers. And, between us, a better one.

Can it look like it used to?

Yes. F2 switches the picture mode, including “TV 2002” — with the same blur you, it turns out, miss.

Can I skip the videos?

Yes. A video — with one press, a cutscene — by holding the button for four seconds, so you don’t skip the story by accident.

Will it run on an old Intel Mac?

No. It’s built for Apple chips; it won’t launch on Intel.

What about a keyboard?

No, gamepad only. A console game on keys is like playing guitar in mittens.

Will the European edition work?

We haven’t tested it yet. The installer will tell you if something’s off.

How do I know if my Mac has an Apple chip?

The apple in the top-left corner → “About This Mac”. If the “Chip” line says Apple — you’re in the club.
