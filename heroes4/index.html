Heroes of Might and Magic IV on Mac

The same “Heroes 4” that made a whole generation skip lectures. Now it lives on your MacBook as a regular Apple Silicon app — at 60 fps, no CrossOver.

Download from GitHub

You need: a Mac with an Apple chip, macOS 26 or newer, and the game from GOG.

What changed

  • Full-screen mapCombat is full-screen too: painted edges instead of black bars.
  • Three times smoother60 frames per second instead of 20.
  • No CrossOver neededCrossOver lists Heroes IV on Mac as “Will Not Install”. Ours is a regular Apple Silicon app.
  • Both expansionsThe Gathering Storm and Winds of War are included.

How to install

What you’ll need

  • A Mac with an Apple chip and macOS 26 or newer
  • The game from GOG: Heroes IV Complete Edition
  • 5 GB of free space while it builds

Step by step

  1. 1Buy the game on GOG.Download the Complete (version 3.0) Windows installer from there and leave it in Downloads. Don’t open it.
  2. 2Download our installer.The button at the top of the page downloads Heroes.IV.dmg, 46 MB. No game inside.
  3. 3Drag “Heroes” to Applications.The first time, right-click it → Open → Open again. The Mac will ask whether you trust us. Trust us.
  4. 4Point it to the GOG installer.If the Mac asks for developer tools, install them. Then the game is built on your Mac: 15–40 minutes depending on the Mac, once. A window shows the progress.
  5. 5Pick a language and play.English or Ukrainian, in the launch window, every time. From then on the game opens right away.

A bit of history

2002. The publisher 3DO is dying, and “Heroes IV” goes out half-baked — like a novel handed in because the debt collectors showed up. Critics spat. Players quietly sat in it for ten years.

For twenty years playing it on a Mac meant a Windows PC nearby or a bottle that wouldn’t install. Now the game is a regular Mac app — on a Mac none of us could even imagine in 2002.

The same game, with the same maps and campaigns. It just doesn’t need a computer from two thousand and two anymore.

FAQ for nerds

The Mac won't open the installer?

Right-click “Heroes” → «Open» → «Open» again. We don't pay Apple for the right to be trusted, so it gets a little nervous.

How much space does it need?

5 GB free while it builds, then about 1.9 GB, English and Ukrainian together.

Can I play Heroes 4 on a Mac?

Yes. The port runs the game as a regular app on any Mac with an Apple chip and macOS 26 or newer.

Isn’t CrossOver or Whisky easier?

It isn’t: CodeWeavers’ own database says Heroes IV on a Mac “Will Not Install” in CrossOver. Our port is a regular app for Apple chips — no Windows layer, no bottles.

Will a copy not from GOG work?

We only tested Complete Edition version 3.0 from GOG — that one works. Another copy may not build; the installer will tell you.

Which macOS do I need?

macOS 26 or newer on a Mac with an Apple chip — M1 or later. Older versions aren’t supported.

Do I need to buy the game?

Yes, the Complete Edition on GOG. We only turn your copy into a Mac version — we don’t have the game itself.

How long does the install take?

The first build takes 15–40 minutes depending on the Mac; a window shows the progress. After that the game opens right away. If Apple’s developer tools aren’t there yet, the installer offers to install them.

Is everything translated?

Almost. All six original campaigns, the tutorial, Winds of War and the whole interface — yes. The Gathering Storm expansion is in English for now; its translation comes later.

Can I play online?

No, we switched it off on purpose: the servers are long dead, and your Mac doesn’t need an extra hole into the internet.

Is there English?

Yes. Pick English or Ukrainian in the launch window, every time. The Gathering Storm expansion is in English for now; its translation comes later.

Is it really free?

Yes. No donations, subscriptions or “premium versions”.

Can I change how the picture looks?

Yes, F10 switches filters: from sharp to smooth, to your taste.

How do I know if my Mac has an Apple chip?

The apple in the top-left corner → “About This Mac”. If the “Chip” line says Apple — you’re in the club.

The Mac won’t open the installer?

Right-click it → “Open” → “Open” again. We don’t pay Apple for the right to be trusted, so it gets a little nervous.
